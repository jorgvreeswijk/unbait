Why this plugin exists

Many news sites use vague, curiosity-driven headlines that hide what an article is actually about. You end up clicking articles that don't interest you and missing ones that do. Unbait reads the article and rewrites the headline so you can tell at a glance whether it's worth your time.

Good to know

AI-generated titles are interpretations, not objective truths. Nuance can be lost, or the AI may emphasize a detail that isn't the main point. You're trading the editor's sensationalism for an algorithm's best guess. The original title is always one click away.

Clickbait exploits the curiosity gap: the space between what you know and what you want to know. A clear title closes that gap, letting you decide calmly whether to read on.

Limitations

Unbait needs to briefly fetch each article to generate a better title. Articles behind a login wall or paywall cannot be read, so those headlines will remain unchanged.

Browser warnings

Your browser may warn that Unbait can "read and modify webpages." This is correct: Unbait needs to read headlines and replace them with better ones. However, Unbait only activates on sites you explicitly enable. It does not run in the background or access any site without your permission. No data is collected, no browsing history is stored, and no information is sent anywhere except directly to your chosen AI provider.
