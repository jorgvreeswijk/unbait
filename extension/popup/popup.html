Anthropic API key

1. Create an account
Go to console.anthropic.com. Sign in with your existing Claude account or create a new developer account.

2. Set up billing
The API uses prepaid credits (separate from a Claude Pro subscription). Go to Settings > Billing, add a payment method and deposit at least $5.

3. Create a key
Go to API Keys, click Create Key, give it a name and copy the key immediately. You'll only see it once.

Cost

Model: Claude Haiku 4.5

News sites: < $0.01 per page · YouTube: < $0.01 per 15 videos

Why Anthropic?

Recommended. Fastest provider (~5s news, ~12s YouTube). Best title quality, especially for non-English headlines. Streaming shows results as they arrive.

OpenAI API key

1. Create an account
Go to platform.openai.com and sign up or log in.

2. Set up billing
Go to Settings > Billing, add a payment method and deposit at least $5.

3. Create a key
Go to API Keys, click Create new secret key and copy it immediately.

Cost

Model: GPT-4o mini

News sites: < $0.01 per page · YouTube: < $0.01 per 15 videos

Why OpenAI?

Good alternative (~7s news, ~15s YouTube). Streaming support. Slightly less accurate on non-English titles than Anthropic.

Google Gemini API key

1. Get a key
Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and create a new API key.

Cost

Model: Gemini 2.5 Flash

Free tier: ~20 pages/day, 5 requests/min

Paid tier: Enable billing in Google AI Studio for unlimited use at comparable cost to Anthropic/OpenAI.

Why Gemini?

Free to try, no credit card required. Slowest of the three (~10s news, ~22s YouTube). May replace fewer titles, especially for non-English content.

Note: Google's API sends your key as a URL parameter (visible in browser dev tools). Anthropic and OpenAI use secure headers instead.

Why this plugin exists

Many news sites use vague, curiosity-driven headlines that hide what an article is actually about. You end up clicking articles that don't interest you and missing ones that do. Unbait reads the article and rewrites the headline so you can tell at a glance whether it's worth your time.

Good to know

AI-generated titles are interpretations, not objective truths. Nuance can be lost, or the AI may emphasize a detail that isn't the main point. You're trading the editor's sensationalism for an algorithm's best guess. The original title is always one click away.

Clickbait exploits the curiosity gap: the space between what you know and what you want to know. A clear title closes that gap, letting you decide calmly whether to read on.

Limitations

Unbait needs to briefly fetch each article to generate a better title. Articles behind a login wall or paywall cannot be read, so those headlines will remain unchanged.

Browser warnings

Your browser may warn that Unbait can "read and modify webpages." This is correct: Unbait needs to read headlines and replace them with better ones. However, Unbait only activates on sites you explicitly enable. It does not run in the background or access any site without your permission. No data is collected, no browsing history is stored, and no information is sent anywhere except directly to your chosen AI provider.

Content processing

Unbait only processes content you already have access to in your browser. Brief excerpts are sent to your AI provider to generate better titles. No original article text or transcripts are stored or redistributed. Only the generated titles are cached locally (7 days).
