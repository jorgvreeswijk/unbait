How it works

1

Scan the page

Unbait finds all article headlines on the page you're viewing.

2

Read the article

For each headline, it briefly fetches the article to understand the actual content.

3

Your AI rewrites

Your own AI rewrites vague or misleading headlines into clear, specific titles. Good headlines stay unchanged.

Features

Always On mode

Enable per-site automatic de-clickbaiting. Headlines are rewritten as soon as you visit the page.

Original always visible

Click the Unbait icon next to any title to toggle back to the original headline.

Privacy first

Your API key stays on your device. Article text is sent directly to your chosen AI provider over HTTPS, never through a third-party server.

You're in control

Bring your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google Gemini. No subscriptions, no middleman, no data collected. You decide which provider and sites to use.

Works on any site

Enable for any news site, tech blog, or content aggregator. You control which sites Unbait runs on.

Selective rewriting

Only clickbait headlines get rewritten. Clear, informative titles are left untouched.

Good to know

AI titles are interpretations

AI-generated titles are the model's best guess, not objective truths. Nuance can get lost, or the AI may emphasize a detail that isn't the main point. You're trading the editor's sensationalism for an algorithm's interpretation. The original title is always one click away.

The curiosity gap

Clickbait exploits the curiosity gap: the space between what you know and what you want to know. A clear title closes that gap, letting you decide calmly whether to read on.

Limitations

Unbait needs to briefly fetch each article. Articles behind login walls or paywalls cannot be read, so those headlines stay unchanged.

Getting started

1. Install the extension

Download from GitHub Releases and load it in Chrome (Settings → Extensions → Load unpacked) or Safari.

2. Get an API key

Choose your provider to see setup instructions:

Model: Claude Haiku 4.5 · Cost: < $0.01 per page

  1. Go to console.anthropic.com and create a developer account (or sign in with your Claude account).
  2. Go to Settings > Billing, add a payment method and deposit at least $5. Note: API billing is separate from a Claude Pro subscription.
  3. Go to API Keys, click Create Key and copy it immediately.

Tip: Create a workspace and set a monthly spending limit under Limits.

Model: GPT-4o mini · Cost: < $0.01 per page

  1. Go to platform.openai.com and sign up or log in.
  2. Go to Settings > Billing, add a payment method and deposit at least $5.
  3. Go to API Keys, click Create new secret key and copy it immediately.

Tip: Set a monthly budget under Settings > Limits to cap your spending.

Model: Gemini 2.0 Flash · Cost: Free tier available (15 req/min)

  1. Go to aistudio.google.com/apikey and create a new API key.
  2. That's it! Gemini has a generous free tier, no billing setup required for light usage.

Tip: The free tier is enough for casual browsing. For heavy use, billing activates automatically.

3. Paste your key and go

Click the Unbait icon in your browser toolbar, select your provider, paste your API key, and click "Unbait!" or enable Always On for your favorite sites.
